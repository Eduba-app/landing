Welcome to Eduba ("we," "us," or "our"). We built Eduba to be a focused, privacy-respecting learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Eduba mobile application, how we use it, and your choices. By using Eduba you agree to the practices described here.

1 Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and the password you choose (stored securely, never in plain text).

Usage data. We automatically collect information about how you interact with the app — books you open, flashcard sessions, streak activity, and features you use — to power your progress tracking and personalized review schedule.

Device & technical data. We collect basic device information (device model, OS version, app version, language setting) and anonymized crash/error reports to keep the app stable.

Purchase data. If you subscribe or make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive a confirmation and subscription status, but never your full payment card details.

Support communications. If you contact us via email or WhatsApp, we keep that conversation to resolve your issue.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Eduba app and its features.
  • Sync your library, flashcards, and progress across your devices.
  • Send you review reminders and streak notifications (you can turn these off in Settings).
  • Process subscriptions and manage access to premium content.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve app performance.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising profiling, or share it with data brokers.

3 Information Sharing

We only share your data in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, authentication, analytics). They may only process your data to provide services to us and are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • App stores: Apple and Google process payments and may receive usage data per their own privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: If Eduba is acquired or merged, your data may transfer as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email or in-app notice before that happens.

4 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. We apply industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest to protect your information.

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach occurs that affects your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 5).

5 Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings.

To delete your account, use our account deletion page. For other rights, contact us at the details in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

6 Children's Privacy

Eduba is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

7 Third-Party Services

The app integrates with third-party services such as:

  • Apple App Store & Google Play (purchases and distribution)
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (data hosting)
  • Analytics tools (anonymized crash reporting)

Each of these has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties outside our control.

8 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Eduba offers optional subscriptions to unlock premium content and features. All billing is handled by Apple or Google through their respective stores. We do not store or have access to your full payment details.

Subscription management, cancellation, and refund requests must be handled directly through the App Store or Google Play according to their policies.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top and, for material changes, notify you via in-app notice or email. Continued use of Eduba after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
